## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design compresses the scene into one memorable spatial symbol. Use abstract architectural contours, geometric planes, a few color modules and symbolic elements. Keep one anchor, generous white space and editorial order. Edges may be slightly irregular, balancing organic geometry and playful minimalism. Reject a realistic full scene, excess detail, a photographic tracing and decorative clutter.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.
